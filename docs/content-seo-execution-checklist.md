# Content SEO Execution Checklist

## Current Read
- Technical SEO is in place: sitemap, robots, manifest, global metadata, and structured data.
- The homepage is the only route with meaningful search targeting today.
- Public routes like `/audit`, `/login`, and legal pages need page-specific metadata.
- There are no search-intent landing pages, FAQ content, or role-based content clusters yet.

## Goal
Turn PivotIQ from a technically crawlable site into a content-led acquisition engine for:
- AI career risk
- job automation risk
- career pivot planning
- AI-safe or AI-resilient careers
- role-specific AI risk searches

## Phase 1: Metadata and Crawl Surface
- Add route-level metadata for `/audit`, `/login`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, and `/contact`.
- Add `noindex` metadata for utility and gated routes like `/dashboard`, `/success`, and `/report`.
- Keep canonicals consistent and aligned with `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL`.
- Verify titles and descriptions in English, Dutch, and German.

## Phase 2: Homepage SEO Rewrite
- Rewrite hero and section headings around a primary search theme:
  - AI career risk scanner
  - job automation risk test
  - career pivot planner
- Add stronger explanatory copy near the top of the page without losing the premium tone.
- Expand crawlable supporting copy for:
  - task-level AI exposure
  - adjacent career pivots
  - transition roadmaps

## Phase 3: FAQ and Rich Snippets
- Add a homepage FAQ section targeting core commercial and informational queries.
- Add FAQ schema for the final FAQ set.
- Focus on questions like:
  - Will AI replace my job?
  - Which white-collar jobs are most exposed to AI?
  - How do I know if I should pivot?
  - What makes a pivot believable?
  - How long does a career pivot usually take?

## Phase 4: Pillar Landing Pages
- Create high-intent landing pages:
  - `/ai-career-risk`
  - `/job-automation-risk`
  - `/career-pivot-planner`
  - `/ai-proof-careers`
  - `/white-collar-jobs-at-risk-from-ai`
- Each page should include:
  - clear search-intent headline
  - concise educational content
  - product explanation
  - FAQ module
  - CTA into `/audit`

## Phase 5: Role-Based SEO Cluster
- Launch the first role pages:
  - marketing manager
  - FP&A analyst
  - HR business partner
  - customer success manager
  - recruiter
  - office manager
- Standard page structure:
  - what AI is changing in the role
  - tasks most exposed to automation
  - durable human leverage
  - believable adjacent pivots
  - CTA to run the scan

## Phase 6: Credibility and Trust Content
- Add a methodology page covering:
  - how scores are formed
  - what “task-level analysis” means
  - why pivots are chosen
- Add a short “how it works” content block on major landing pages.
- Keep privacy, terms, and contact internally linked from all pillar pages.

## Phase 7: Internal Linking System
- Homepage links to pillar pages.
- Pillar pages link to role pages.
- Role pages link back to relevant pillar pages.
- Every content page should drive toward `/audit`.
- Footer should reinforce trust-page crawl depth.

## Phase 8: Measurement and Iteration
- Connect Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Track:
  - impressions
  - CTR
  - top landing pages
  - top queries
  - conversion rate from content page to `/audit`
- Review query data weekly and expand content based on real demand.

## Recommended Build Order
1. Phase 1 metadata and crawl cleanup
2. Homepage SEO rewrite
3. FAQ section and FAQ schema
4. Three pillar pages
5. First six role pages
6. Methodology page
7. Internal linking pass
8. Search Console iteration loop

## Definition of Done for the First SEO Wave
- Every public marketing route has route-specific metadata.
- Utility or account routes are not indexable.
- Homepage targets a clear primary query set.
- At least three pillar pages are live.
- At least three role pages are live.
- FAQ schema is valid.
- Internal links guide crawlers and users into `/audit`.
