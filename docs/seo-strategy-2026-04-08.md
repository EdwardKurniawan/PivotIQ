# PivotIQ SEO Strategy

## Goal
Win search traffic from high-intent white-collar professionals who are actively looking for:
- AI career risk answers
- job automation risk by role
- career pivot planning
- role-specific AI risk guidance

The realistic path to page-one and top-position gains is not broad vanity terms first. It is:
1. own long-tail problem keywords
2. own role-specific AI risk pages
3. build internal links from pillar pages to role pages
4. expand clusters around proven role families

## On-page strategy

### Homepage
Primary target:
- ai career risk scanner

Secondary support:
- career pivot planner
- job automation risk

Role:
- brand + conversion hub
- should not try to rank for every term

### Pillar pages
Use them as cluster hubs:
- `/ai-career-risk` links to role guides and audit
- `/career-pivot-planner` links to pivot-planning and role pages
- `/white-collar-jobs-at-risk-from-ai` links to role guides by function

### Role pages
Each role page should:
- lead with exact-match role keyword in title/H1/meta
- explain exposed tasks, protected leverage, and adjacent pivots
- link to the free scan
- link to at least 2 adjacent role guides

## Internal linking rules

1. Every pillar page should link to:
- the other two pillar pages
- 3 relevant role guides
- the free scan

2. Every role page should link to:
- the free scan
- methodology
- 3 related role guides
- at least 1 pillar page

3. Homepage should keep role-guide links visible near the fold and near the footer.
